## Challenge

The Telegraph's product team was disappointed with the look of podcast pages and
sought to improve the design and user experience.

The goal was to continue using the template and podcast service, but to take
advantage of all the available features on the page and of the third party's
APIs.

## Solution

After researching the page template and API documentation, I worked with
designers to create concepts that were technically feasible.

I created a custom HTML embed with in-house styles that matched the page
surroundings better. The podcast artwork was featured more prominently and was
also used as a blurred background image.

I also created a dashboard tool that contained all existing podcast series. The
editors were able to choose the series they wanted to embed, either as a single
episode or a whole playlist, and then copy the embed HTML code directly to the
clipboard.

The feedback from editors and department heads was positive, and the prototype
was finished within a week.
